This notice is for residents of California. It supplements our Privacy Policy and explains what the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and its regulations (together, the “CCPA”) require us to tell you. Words defined in the CCPA have the same meaning here.

The CCPA applies to businesses that meet certain thresholds. Whether or not it applies to Tru Leaf International LLC (“Tru Leaf”, “we”, “us”) at any given time, we follow this notice for California residents. [Counsel to confirm whether the CCPA applies to Tru Leaf.]

1. Notice at collection

This section is our notice at collection. It tells you, at or before the point we collect personal information, the categories we collect, why we collect them, whether we sell or share them, and how long we keep them.

  • We do not sell personal information for money. If you accept advertising cookies, the Meta Pixel sends Meta Platforms, Inc. identifiers (a cookie ID and your IP address) and internet activity (the pages you view). This is “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising and may be a “sale” under the CCPA. We do it only with your consent. You can opt out at any time on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page or in Cookie Choices, and we honor Global Privacy Control. [Counsel to confirm.]
  • We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose other than those the CCPA permits, such as considering a job application, performing the services you ask for and keeping our systems secure. We do not use it to infer characteristics about you.
  • How long we keep it depends on the category and purpose, and is set out in the table below and in section 11 of our Privacy Policy.

3. Business and commercial purposes

We collect, use and disclose personal information for these business and commercial purposes:

  • responding to inquiries and providing the information, samples, quotes and products you ask for;
  • assessing applications from retailers, distributors and white-label customers, and verifying that they are licensed;
  • managing customer and supplier relationships, orders, deliveries and payments;
  • recruiting and hiring;
  • helping to ensure security and integrity, and detecting and responding to security incidents, fraud and illegal activity;
  • debugging to identify and repair errors in the site;
  • maintaining the quality of the site and our services, including measuring how the site is used, with your consent;
  • marketing on Facebook and Instagram, with your consent;
  • complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests; and
  • establishing, exercising and defending legal claims.

4. Your rights under the CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights.

  • Right to know and access. You can ask us to tell you the categories of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, our business or commercial purposes, the categories of third parties to which we disclosed it, and the specific pieces of personal information we hold about you. You can ask for this for the period beginning January 1, 2022, unless doing so would be impossible or require disproportionate effort.
  • Right to delete. You can ask us to delete personal information we collected from you, subject to exceptions the CCPA allows, for example where we need it to complete a transaction, keep our systems secure, comply with a legal obligation or exercise legal claims.
  • Right to correct. You can ask us to correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to opt out of sale and sharing. You can tell us not to sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Turn off advertising in Cookie Choices, send a request on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page, or turn on Global Privacy Control, which we honor as a valid opt-out.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We use sensitive personal information only for purposes the CCPA permits without offering this right, so we do not offer a separate link to limit its use. If that changes, we will add one.
  • Right to non-discrimination and no retaliation. We will not discriminate against you for using any of these rights, and we will not retaliate against a job applicant, employee or contractor for using them.

5. How to use your rights

You can make a request in any of these ways:

  • use the form on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page;
  • email distributors@truleaf-tobacco.com with “California privacy request” in the subject line; or
  • call us at +1 (645) 243-9880. [Add a toll-free number here if counsel advises one is required.]

We will confirm receipt of a request to know, delete or correct within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days. If we need more time, we may take up to another 45 days and will tell you why. We will act on a request to opt out of sale or sharing as soon as we can and no later than 15 business days after we receive it.

Verification. Before we act on a request to know, delete or correct, we verify your identity by matching information you give us with information we already hold, usually your name and the email address you used with us. For specific pieces of personal information, or for sensitive information, we will ask for more and may require a signed declaration under penalty of perjury. We do not need to verify your identity for an opt-out request, but we may decline one we believe is fraudulent and will tell you why.

Authorized agents. You can use an authorized agent to make a request for you. Unless the agent has a valid power of attorney under the California Probate Code, we will ask the agent for signed permission from you, and we may ask you to verify your identity directly with us or confirm that you gave the agent permission.

Household requests and minors. We do not maintain household profiles. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21, and we do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

6. Notice to California job applicants

This section is our notice at collection for people who apply for a job or contract role with us from California.

What we collect. We collect the information you give us in your application and resume, including identifiers (your name, email address, phone number and city), professional and employment-related information (your experience, current or most recent job, the role and type of work you want and when you could start), education information you choose to include, your LinkedIn profile if you give it, and sensitive personal information about whether you are authorized to work in the United States and need visa sponsorship. If we interview you, we also keep notes of the interview and of reference checks you authorize.

Why we collect it. To assess your application, communicate with you, check references, decide whether to make an offer, comply with employment, immigration and anti-discrimination laws, keep records of our hiring process, and establish or defend legal claims.

Who we disclose it to. Our service providers, such as our website host and form processor and our email provider, and our professional advisers. We do not sell or share the information in your application.

How long we keep it. [4 years] after we make a decision about your application, to meet employment record-keeping requirements and defend legal claims. If we hire you, your application becomes part of your personnel file and you will receive a separate notice for employees.

Applicants have the same rights as other California residents, described in section 4, and can use them in the same ways.

7. Shine the Light

California Civil Code section 1798.83 lets California residents ask for information about the personal information a business has disclosed to third parties for those third parties’ direct marketing purposes. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.
