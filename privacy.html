This Privacy Policy explains how Tru Leaf International LLC collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information when you visit truleaf-tobacco.com, contact us, apply to sell our products, apply for our white-label program or apply for a job with us. It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Please read it together with our Terms of Use. If you live in California, our California Privacy Notice gives further detail required by California law.

1. Who we are

Tru Leaf International LLC (“Tru Leaf”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a Florida limited liability company based in Miami, Florida. We grow and cure tobacco leaf in Nicaragua and sell tobacco leaf wraps, whole tobacco leaf, natural cigar blunts and tobacco cones to licensed retailers and distributors in the United States.

Tru Leaf is the business responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us using the details in section 23.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to personal information we collect:

  • through this website and every page on it (the “site”);
  • through the forms on the site, including the wholesale inquiry, white-label application, contact, careers and privacy request forms;
  • when you email, call or message us, including through our Instagram account; and
  • when we deal with you as a current or prospective retailer, distributor, white-label customer, supplier or job applicant.

It does not apply to websites, apps or services run by other companies, even if the site links to them. Those companies have their own privacy policies.

“Personal information” means information that identifies, relates to or could reasonably be linked with you or your household. It does not include information that has been de-identified or aggregated so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to you, or information that is lawfully made available to the public.

3. Personal information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the site and how you deal with us. We collect only what we need for the purposes described in section 4.

Information you give us

  • Wholesale inquiries. Your name, company, email address, phone number, business type, city and state, the product families you are interested in, and anything you write in your message.
  • White-label applications. Your company name, your name, email address and phone number, business type, website or social media account, business address, and, if you choose to give them, your business tax ID (EIN) and tobacco license number. We also collect the products you want, your estimated first order, the status of your branding, whether you want help with packaging design or distribution, and anything you write in your message. Please use your business EIN and never a Social Security number.
  • Contact messages. Your name, email address, phone number if you give it, the topic of your message and what you write.
  • Job applications. Your name, email address, phone number, the city you live in, your LinkedIn profile, the role and type of work you want, when you could start, whether you are authorized to work in the United States and whether you would need visa sponsorship, your experience, your current or most recent job, what you tell us about yourself, your resume and anything it contains, and how you heard about us.
  • Privacy requests. Your name, email address, postal address, the choices you make and, if you act for someone else, information about your authority to do so.
  • Business dealings. If we go on to work together, we collect the information needed to run that relationship, such as names and job titles of contacts, delivery and billing addresses, license and tax registration details, orders, invoices and correspondence.
  • Other communications. Anything you send us by email, phone, text or direct message, including attachments and artwork.

Please do not send us sensitive information we have not asked for, such as health information, Social Security numbers, financial account numbers or information about other people without their permission.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and security logs. When your browser loads a page, our hosting provider receives technical information as a matter of course: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser type, device type and operating system. From the IP address it may be possible to infer an approximate location, such as a city or region. We use these logs to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems.
  • Information stored in your browser. The site stores a small number of records in your own browser so that it works properly. They are described in section 7.
  • Analytics, only if you agree. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how you use the site: the pages you view, how you arrived, how long you stay, scrolling and clicks on links, your approximate location (city or region), your device, browser and operating system, and a cookie identifier for your browser.
  • Advertising, only if you agree. If you accept advertising cookies, the Meta Pixel sends Meta Platforms, Inc. the pages you view, together with a cookie identifier, your IP address and browser details. Meta may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account.

Neither tool runs until you accept it in the cookie banner or in Cookie Choices, and you can turn either off at any time. They do not record what you type into our forms. The site does not use session recording, keystroke logging or fingerprinting.

Information from other sources

  • Public and licensing records. To confirm that we sell only to licensed tobacco businesses, we may check state and local license records, business registrations and other public sources about your business.
  • References and recruiting sources. If you apply for a job, we may receive information from people who refer you, from references you name, and from public professional profiles you point us to.
  • Business partners. Distributors and sales representatives may pass us contact details of retailers who want to stock our products.
  • Social media. If you interact with our Instagram account, we see the information Instagram makes available to us under its own terms, such as your handle, your public profile and your messages to us.

4. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • reply to your messages and inquiries and give you the information you ask for;
  • assess wholesale, distribution and white-label applications, including checking that a business is licensed to buy and sell tobacco products;
  • prepare quotes, samples and proposals, and set up and manage customer and supplier relationships, orders, deliveries, invoices and payments;
  • produce white-label products to your specifications, including designing your packaging, handling your artwork and labelling;
  • if you ask, introduce you to distributors and retailers that may sell your white-label products;
  • help people find stores that carry our products;
  • consider you for a job, contact you about your application, check your references and keep records of our hiring decisions;
  • keep the site, our systems and our business secure, and detect, prevent and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents and unlawful activity;
  • confirm that visitors are 21 or older and keep our marketing directed to adults;
  • comply with the law, including tobacco licensing, labelling, tax, record-keeping and employment laws, and respond to lawful requests from public authorities;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims and enforce our agreements;
  • maintain and improve the site and our products and services;
  • if you accept analytics cookies, measure how the site is used so we can improve it; and
  • if you accept advertising cookies, measure and improve our marketing on Facebook and Instagram.

We may also send business contacts information about our products, such as new formats or availability. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying to the message or contacting us. We do not send marketing to consumers through this site.

If we want to use personal information for a purpose that is materially different from those listed here, we will tell you first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent.

5. How we disclose personal information

We disclose personal information only as described here.

  • Service providers. Companies that work for us under contract and may use the information only to provide their services to us. These include our website host and form processor (currently Netlify, Inc.), our email and office software provider, our font provider (Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads), and providers of storage, IT, security, shipping, accounting and payment services.
  • Analytics provider, with your consent. Google LLC, which provides Google Analytics, receives information about your visit if you accept analytics cookies. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalization for it, so it is used to measure the site only.
  • Advertising platform, with your consent. Meta Platforms, Inc. receives information about your visit through the Meta Pixel if you accept advertising cookies. Meta may use it for its own purposes under its own policies, including advertising. Section 6 explains what this means under state privacy laws.
  • Professional advisers. Our lawyers, accountants, auditors and insurers, where they need the information to advise or protect us.
  • Manufacturing and logistics partners. For white-label orders, the farms, factories, printers and carriers that make, pack and ship your products, to the extent they need your specifications, artwork and delivery details.
  • At your direction. Other parties you ask us to share information with, or that you allow us to share with, such as a distributor that serves your area.
  • Legal and safety reasons. Courts, regulators, law enforcement and other public authorities when we believe the law requires it, and others when we believe disclosure is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Tru Leaf, our customers, our staff or the public, or to enforce our terms.
  • Business transfers. A buyer, investor, successor or other party in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale of all or part of our business or assets, including during negotiations. Personal information would remain subject to this policy or to a policy that is at least as protective.
  • Affiliates. Companies under common ownership or control with Tru Leaf, which will handle it in line with this policy.

8. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

Global Privacy Control. Some browsers and extensions can send a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal to tell websites you do not want your personal information sold or shared. We treat a GPC signal as a valid request to opt out of the sale and sharing of personal information for that browser, and for any account or identity we can link to that browser. When the site detects the signal, it does not load the Meta Pixel, even if advertising cookies were accepted before.

Do Not Track. Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no common industry standard for how to respond to it, so the site does not respond to it. To stop analytics or advertising, use Cookie Choices or Global Privacy Control.

9. Location information

The Find a Store page has a “Use my location” button. If you press it, your browser asks for your permission before sharing your location with the page. If you allow it, your location is used inside your browser only, to sort the list of stores by distance. It is not sent to us or stored.

You can also search by city, state or ZIP code instead. What you type is processed in your browser in the same way.

10. Social media and links to other sites

The site links to our Instagram account and shows a preview of it. The preview is a set of images stored on our site. It does not load anything from Instagram until you follow a link. Once you visit Instagram, or any other site we link to, that company’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other companies.

When you follow us, message us or comment on our posts on Instagram, Meta Platforms, Inc. collects and handles that information under its own terms. We can see what Instagram shows account holders and may keep copies of messages you send us so we can reply.

11. How long we keep personal information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, unless the law requires or permits us to keep it longer. In deciding how long to keep it, we consider what it is, why we have it, the risk of harm from keeping it, and our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

  • Inquiries and messages that do not lead to a business relationship: [24 months] after our last contact with you.
  • White-label applications that do not go ahead: [24 months] after our last contact with you.
  • Customer and supplier records, including orders, invoices, license details and correspondence: for the life of the relationship and then [7 years], to meet tax, accounting and tobacco record-keeping rules.
  • Job applications: [4 years] after the hiring decision, to meet employment record-keeping rules and defend legal claims.
  • Privacy requests: at least 24 months, so we can show how we handled them.
  • Server and security logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them, which is usually a short period unless a log is needed to investigate a security incident.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics keeps it for [14 months] and then deletes it.
  • Meta Pixel data: Meta keeps it under its own data policy.
  • Cookie choices: 12 months, after which the site asks again.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it or de-identify it so it can no longer be linked to you.

12. How we protect personal information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards suited to the information we hold. The site is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Form submissions are stored by our hosting provider and forwarded to a limited number of people at Tru Leaf who need them for their work. We limit who can access personal information and require our service providers to protect it.

No website, transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us. Please do not email us information you would not want intercepted.

If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires it, including under Florida’s data security law (section 501.171 of the Florida Statutes) and the laws of the state where you live.

13. Age requirement and children’s privacy

The site and our products are only for adults 21 and older. The site asks visitors to confirm they are 21 or older before showing them any content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21, and the site is not directed to children under 13 within the meaning of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act.

If you believe someone under 21 has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information. Some rights apply only if the privacy law of your state applies to Tru Leaf, which depends on the size of our business and how much personal information we handle. We will honor the requests described here from anyone in the United States wherever we reasonably can, even if the law does not require us to.

  • Access. To confirm whether we hold personal information about you, and to receive a copy of it.
  • Portability. To receive personal information you gave us in a portable, commonly used format.
  • Correction. To correct personal information that is inaccurate.
  • Deletion. To have us delete personal information about you, subject to exceptions, for example where we must keep it by law or need it to complete a transaction you asked for.
  • Opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising. To opt out of the sale of your personal information, its sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and its use for targeted advertising. The only activity of this kind on the site is the Meta Pixel, which runs only if you accept advertising cookies. You can turn it off in Cookie Choices.
  • Opt out of profiling. To opt out of profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make decisions of that kind using automated processing.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. To limit our use of sensitive personal information. We use it only for the purposes the law permits, as explained in our California Privacy Notice.
  • Know about disclosures. In some states, to receive a list of the categories of third parties, or the specific third parties, to which we have disclosed personal information.
  • Appeal. To appeal our decision if we decline to act on your request.
  • Non-discrimination. Not to be treated differently for using any of these rights.

15. How to make a request

You can make a request in any of these ways:

  • use the form on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page, which also accepts access, correction and deletion requests, or change your choices in Cookie Choices;
  • email distributors@truleaf-tobacco.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line; or
  • call us at +1 (645) 243-9880. [Add a toll-free number here if counsel advises one is required.]

Please tell us what you are asking for and give us enough information to find the records that relate to you, such as the name, email address and company you used when you contacted us.

Verifying your identity

To protect you, we need to verify your identity before we give out or delete personal information. We do this by matching the information you give us with what we already hold, usually your name and the email address you used with us. For requests about more sensitive information, we may ask for more details. We will use the information you give us for verification only to verify your request, and we will delete it once we have finished with it, unless we need to keep it as a record of the request. You do not need to verify your identity to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Authorized agents

You can ask someone else to make a request for you. We will ask the agent for proof that you have authorized them, such as signed written permission or a valid power of attorney, and we may also ask you to verify your own identity with us directly or to confirm that you gave the agent permission.

How long we take

We will confirm that we have received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days. If we need more time, we may extend that period by up to another 45 days, and we will tell you why. We do not charge for requests unless they are manifestly unfounded, excessive or repetitive, in which case we may charge a reasonable fee or decline them, and we will explain our reasons.

Appeals

If we decline your request, in whole or in part, we will tell you why. You can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing us with “Privacy appeal” in the subject line within a reasonable time. We will respond to your appeal in writing within 60 days, or sooner if the law of your state requires it, and explain the outcome. If you are not satisfied with our response to your appeal, you can contact the attorney general of your state.

16. No discrimination or retaliation

We will not deny you goods or services, charge you a different price, give you a different level of quality, or otherwise treat you differently because you used your privacy rights. We will not retaliate against a job applicant or employee for using these rights.

17. California residents

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, the “CCPA”), may give you the rights described in section 14. Our California Privacy Notice contains our notice at collection, the categories of personal information we collect and disclose, and how we handle the personal information of job applicants.

Shine the Light. California Civil Code section 1798.83 lets California residents ask for information about the personal information a business has disclosed to third parties for those third parties’ own direct marketing. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

18. Nevada residents

Nevada law gives Nevada residents the right to opt out of the sale of certain covered information that a website operator collects about them. We do not sell covered information as Nevada law defines it. You can still send us an opt-out request using any of the methods in section 15, and we will record it.

19. Residents of other states with privacy laws

A growing number of states have comprehensive consumer privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia. Each law has its own scope and thresholds. Where one applies to us and to you, you have the rights it gives you, which may include the rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal information, to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling, and to appeal our decisions. Use the methods in section 15 to make a request.

Some of these laws treat certain information as “sensitive data”, which can include information revealing citizenship or immigration status. Where the law requires consent before we process sensitive data, we will ask for it. Our careers form asks whether you are authorized to work in the United States and whether you would need visa sponsorship. We ask only so we know whether we could employ you, and we use the answer for no other purpose.
